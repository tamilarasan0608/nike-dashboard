The Tariff Is The Whole Problem
At $6.42 per pair, the Vietnam tariff is Nike's single largest variable cost — bigger than raw materials ($4.00), bigger than labor ($6.00 minus tariff). It's a pure wealth transfer to the government with zero value returned. Contract manufacturing eliminates it entirely.
🧮
The USMCA Arbitrage: Spend $2.60 to Save $6.42
Adding US final assembly costs $2.60/pair. But it unlocks USMCA exemption, eliminating $6.42 in tariffs. Net gain: $3.82 per pair — just from this trade. That's a 147% return on the US assembly investment.
🚚
Shipping: Truck vs Ocean — Not Just Cheaper, Faster
Vietnam ocean freight costs $1.20/pair and takes 3–4 weeks. Mexico truck costs $0.50/pair and takes 2–3 days. That's $0.70/pair saved plus a 10× improvement in lead time — meaning Nike carries 90% less inventory buffer. Working capital freed: significant.
⚡
Total Impact: $6.27 Saved Per Pair × 30 Million Pairs
Vietnam: $22.82. Contract: $16.55. Difference: $6.27/pair. At 30M pairs annually, that's $188.1M in direct per-pair savings PLUS $125.5M in eliminated tariff exposure = $313.6M total annual benefit.
