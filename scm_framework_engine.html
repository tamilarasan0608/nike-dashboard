Framework 02 · Fisher 1997
02

Product / Service Type

Is your product FUNCTIONAL (stable, predictable) or INNOVATIVE (trendy, unpredictable)? This determines Lean vs Agile strategy.

Type 1 — Functional
Stable, predictable demand. Consistent year-round. Price competition intense. Low margin, high volume.

→ Use LEAN Supply Chain
Type 2 — Innovative
Trendy, unpredictable demand. Seasonal spikes. First-mover advantage critical. High margin, low volume.

→ Use AGILE Supply Chain

Framework 06 · The 4 Powers
06

Risk Mitigation — The 4 Powers

For each top risk, choose your mitigation strategy. Then set your overall portfolio mix to 100%.

Robustness
Be so strong disruptions don't hurt. Buffer stock, quality systems, staff training.
Redundancy
Have backup options. Dual sourcing, multiple sites, safety stock.
Flexibility
Adapt quickly. Modular design, flexible contracts, cross-trained staff.
Transfer
Pay someone else to carry the risk. Insurance, contracts, price hedging.
